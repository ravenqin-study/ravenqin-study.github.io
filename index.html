essay

On the art of staring at residual plots

What it means to spend three hours looking at dots and calling it research.

Every model is a story you tell about the data. The residuals are the parts of the data that refuse to go along with the story.

I used to treat them as noise to be minimised. Now I read them more like marginal comments from a patient but slightly annoyed editor: here you were too confident, here you forgot something, here you simply got it wrong.

Three hours of staring is how you learn to hear them.

The librarian who returned every book

A 500-word story about a library where the books borrow the readers.

Nobody in town remembered when the library started lending people out. It began quietly: a man went in for a book on bridges and came out three weeks later, knowing things about rivers he could not explain.

The librarian kept a card for every reader, stamped with the dates the books had borrowed them. Some came back changed. Some came back overdue.
